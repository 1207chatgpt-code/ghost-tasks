# Ghost Tasks

Ghost Tasks is a small, local-first productivity app for reviving abandoned tasks.

- Keep the first version usable without accounts or paid APIs.
- Use clear language: tasks are added, revived, focused, and released.
- Preserve task data in browser localStorage.
- Maintain a calm, atmospheric dark interface with high contrast and keyboard focus states.
- The app is intended for the Kiroween 2026 Costume Contest.
